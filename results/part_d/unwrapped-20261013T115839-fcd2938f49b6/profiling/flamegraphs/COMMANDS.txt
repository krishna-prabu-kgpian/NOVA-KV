Each <id>.record.log starts with the exact perf record command. Then:
  DEBUGINFOD_URLS= perf script --no-inline -i <id>.data   (kept as <id>.perf-script.txt.xz)
  sed: label unresolved kernel frames [kernel]; stackcollapse-perf.pl; merge consecutive [kernel] frames; flamegraph.pl
Samples are taken on the cycles event: box widths are shares of on-CPU cycles. A thread blocked
in the kernel consumes no cycles and is not sampled. kptr_restrict is nonzero, so kernel frames
have no symbol names; no sample is added, removed or reattributed.
