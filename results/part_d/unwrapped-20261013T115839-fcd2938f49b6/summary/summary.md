## D.1 — point-get scaling, track C: writer ACTIVE during the measured window

`benchmarks/concurrent_bench.cpp`, the generator's schema with every requested key present (hit rate 100 %
unless stated), one writer thread overwriting existing trades for the whole window.

**Timing track: every executable was started directly, with no profiler or other wrapper.** The two columns
marked *(prof.)* are perf stat counters from the separate profiling track (other runs of the same commands);
throughput and latency are never taken from those runs.

**Lock placement.** The two lock-based stores run at two different speeds depending on where their lock object
lies inside its 64-byte cache line (decision D-072). Every such cell is therefore measured twice, with the lock
placed at offset 48 (its first 16 bytes end one line, the rest begins the next) and at offset 0 (cache-line
aligned; offsets 16 and 32 behaved like 0 in the diagnostic). Neither is better everywhere: see the tables.
The RCU store's readers take no lock, so it has one row.
